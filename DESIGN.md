# Design System: Vestígio

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Lit Case Room"**

The page is a dark room lit from outside. A near-black ground sits under fixed, slow ambient light fields of deep navy and royal blue that stay put while content scrolls over them; there is no texture, grid or grain, only light. Objects in the room are glass: double-bezel cards (a thin translucent shell holding a gradient core with concentric corners), pill buttons that carry a smaller icon orb inside them, a floating island nav, and navy icon discs with pale-blue glyphs. Hierarchy comes from light and material, not from rules and boxes.

Color is spent in whole fields rather than sprinkled. Navy owns entire territories (the case-type ticker strip, the rounded About band, the lead problem tile, the mobile menu, the "never passwords" warning) and brings its own blue-tinted ink. The royal accent fills actions, the selected tab pill, lit process steps, the feature principle, the finished report and, once, the entire final call-to-action block. Glow is rationed to one place: the hero's primary WhatsApp button.

Motion is the room's evidence that work is happening: the headline rises word by word, the dossier cycles through its analysis (queued, analyzing, link found) under a soft scan, the cards respond to the pointer, the step rail fills as you scroll, and redaction bars lift off key phrases. Everything that moves turns off under reduced motion; color and opacity states remain. Confirmed rejections from the brand: hacker cliché (green code, glitch, skulls, padlocks), neon, flashy gradients, hooded-figure stock, a law-office serif look, a cold bank look, and (from the v1 review) grid textures and a static text-only hero.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Fixed ambient light fields of navy and accent over #141619; no texture or grain.
- Double-bezel glass cards with concentric radii (28px shell, 6px bezel, 22px core).
- Pill-shaped everything interactive: buttons with nested icon orbs, chips, tabs, island nav, floating WhatsApp.
- Whole-color fields: navy ticker, navy rounded band, solid accent final block.
- Navy icon discs with accent-ink glyphs as the recurring icon container.
- Archivo at 112% width for headings; Martian Mono only for case ids, step and tab numbers.
- Motion is narrative (analysis loop, scroll-lit steps, redaction lifts) and fully disabled under reduced motion.

## Colors

The five pinned brand colors plus derived tints: a graphite ground, glassy graphite surfaces, an institutional navy used as whole fields, one royal-blue action color with a pale-blue ink tint, and WhatsApp green on its icon only.

### Primary
- **Case Blue** (accent): primary buttons, the sliding selected-tab pill, lit step numbers, the open accordion disc, the "we" node in the relay, principle and guide badges, the finished report bar, outgoing chat bubbles, the feature principle and the whole final CTA core. Hover lifts to **Case Blue Lit** (accent-hover). Labels on it use **Accent Paper** (on-accent).
- **Pale Trace Ink** (accent-ink): the AA-legible tint of the accent for text-sized use: links, the "rastro." highlight in the H1, keyboard focus outline, every glyph inside navy discs, chip and seal icons, and, at 0.16 to 0.55 alpha, the hairline borders of navy and accent elements.
- **Hero Glow** (glow-accent): the colored shadow under the hero primary button only.

### Secondary
- **Institutional Navy** (navy): whole fields and icon discs. Fields: ticker strip, About band, lead problem tile, the "never passwords" warning, mobile menu glass (0.88 alpha). Discs: service, sheet, step and list-number icons. It also forms the ambient light fields behind everything.
- **Navy-scoped ink** (navy-text-body, navy-text-muted, navy-text-faint): inside navy fields the grey ladder is replaced by blue-tinted equivalents. In code these are scoped overrides of `--text-body`, `--text-muted`, `--text-faint` on the navy container; the variable names do not change. Navy-text-muted also colors ticker items and the mobile-menu note.
- **Navy Redaction Bar** (navy-redact-bar): the redaction bar inside navy fields, via `--redact-bar`. Elsewhere the bar is surface-hover; inside the accent CTA it is a lighter blue.

### Neutral
- **Case Room Ground** (bg): page ground under the light fields; step number discs at rest.
- **Glass Graphite** (surface) to **Graphite Shade** (surface-2): the top-to-bottom gradient of every bezel core and the sheet panel; surface alone fills lit steps and chip hover. **Graphite Lit** (surface-hover) is the floating button hover and the default redaction bar.
- **Title Ink** (text): headings, strong text, the light button fill. **Body Ink** (text-body): paragraphs. **Muted Ink** (text-muted): secondary copy, nav links, inactive tabs, the second line of the H1. **Faint Ink** (text-faint): queued analysis states, receipt labels, legal footer.
- **Hairline** (hairline) and **Strong Hairline** (hairline-strong): bezel shells, dividers, chip and ghost borders, the step rail track.
- **WhatsApp Green** (whatsapp): the WhatsApp glyph fill only.

### Named Rules
**The Whole-Field Rule.** Navy and the accent are spent as territories (a strip, a band, a tile, a block, a disc, a pill), never as thin decorative lines or text tints. Text-sized blue is accent-ink.

**The Single Glow Rule.** Exactly one element glows: the hero primary CTA (`glow-accent`). Every other shadow is a dark navy-black diffusion.

**The Scoped Ink Rule.** Any navy field redefines `--text-body`, `--text-muted`, `--text-faint` and `--redact-bar` to its blue-tinted values; grey ink never sits on navy.

**The Single Green Rule.** WhatsApp green fills the WhatsApp icon and nothing else.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Archivo (with system-ui, -apple-system, Segoe UI, sans-serif)
**Body Font:** Archivo (same stack)
**Label/Mono Font:** Martian Mono (with ui-monospace, Cascadia Mono, Consolas, monospace)

**Character:** One semi-expanded grotesque carries the voice; headings stretch to 112% and tighten hard, which reads confident and modern without going corporate. The monospace is a case stamp, small and uppercase, never a voice.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (600, 112% width, clamp 40 to 64px, 1.0, -0.04em): the hero H1 only, three lines on desktop, second sentence on its own line in muted ink, the key word in accent-ink.
- **Headline** (600, 112% width, clamp 32 to 56px, 1.08, -0.035em): section titles; the final CTA scales it up to 68px in white; mobile menu links use it at 32 to 48px.
- **Title** (600, 112% width, clamp 20 to 26px, -0.015em): sub-heads, guide titles, sheet title, team title.
- **Card title** (600, 19px, 1.22): service cards, steps, principles. Drops to 16px in compact mobile service rows.
- **Small head** (600, 106% width, 14px): button labels, sheet section heads, analysis title, relay names.
- **Body lead** (400, clamp 17 to 20px, 1.6): section intros, hero lead, FAQ questions (500), contact values.
- **Body** (400, 16px, 1.6): paragraphs; answers cap at 62ch.
- **Body small** (500 for UI, 400 for copy; 14px, 1.55): nav, chips, tabs, seals, card summaries.
- **Label** (Martian Mono 500, 11px, 0.06em, uppercase, tabular numerals): "Caso 01" ids, step numbers 01 to 05, tab counts, numbered list discs.

### Named Rules
**The Pinned Mono Rule.** Martian Mono sets case ids, step numbers and tab counts only (pinned by the brief). It never sets headings, body, buttons, labels above sections or data values.

**The Stretch Rule.** Headings use 112% width; buttons and small heads 106%; the ticker 108%; body stays at 100%.

## Layout

Mobile-first single column in a centered container (76rem plus gutters of 1rem, 1.5rem from 640px, 2.5rem from 1024px). Sections breathe on a generous fluid rhythm (section-y, 88 to 160px); section heads cap at 44rem with a fluid 40 to 64px gap to content. Spacing is a 4px-based scale (space-1 to space-9).

Desktop compositions are asymmetric: hero 1.2fr text / 1fr dossier cascade, the problem section as a 12-column bento (7-column navy lead tile spanning two rows, 5-column side tiles, a full-width relay tile), process 5fr / 7fr and FAQ 4fr / 7fr with the narrow intro sticky under the floating nav, guide 1.12fr / 1fr. The About band is inset from the viewport edges (8 to 24px) with large rounded corners so it reads as an object, not a stripe.

Breakpoints in use: 480 (hero buttons go full width below it), 640 (two-column services, nav CTA appears, dossier back papers appear), 768 (sheet becomes a floating side panel), 900 (bento and relay go horizontal, guide splits), 1024 (desktop nav, split hero at up to 100dvh, three-column services, sticky intros).

**The Compact Row Rule.** Below 640px service cards collapse into compact rows (navy disc, name, arrow orb; summary and case id hidden; 4px bezel, 22px shell) so all eighteen services fit in about one and a half screens.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is light and glass. The ground is lit by fixed radial fields; cards are translucent shells with a 1px inner top highlight; floating objects (island nav when scrolled, dossier papers, the analysis card, the sheet, the WhatsApp button) take soft, wide, navy-black shadows and backdrop blur. Shadows are never pure black and never hard-edged.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Inner highlight** (`box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, `--highlight`): every bezel core, the island nav, principles, the sheet.
- **Soft** (`box-shadow: 0 30px 60px -30px rgba(3, 6, 30, 0.75)`, `--shadow-soft`): scrolled island nav, dossier papers.
- **Float** (`box-shadow: 0 40px 80px -24px rgba(3, 6, 40, 0.8), 0 12px 24px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.4)`, `--shadow-float`): the analysis card and the floating WhatsApp button.
- **Button lift** (`box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,0.18), 0 10px 24px -12px rgba(3,6,30,0.7)`): primary buttons.
- **Hero glow** (`box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,0.18), 0 12px 32px -12px rgba(10,33,192,0.55)`): the hero primary CTA only.
- **Halo ring** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 6px rgba(10, 33, 192, 0.16)`): lit step numbers and the relay "we" node.

### Named Rules
**The Light-Not-Texture Rule.** Atmosphere comes from radial light fields (navy and accent) only. No grid, grain, noise or pattern on any surface.

## Shapes

Round and concentric. Containers use the double bezel: a 28px shell, a 6px translucent bezel, and a 22px core so inner and outer corners stay concentric (the final CTA thickens to 8px and up to 44px). Mid-size glass panels without a shell use 18 to 24px (steps and contact items 22px, principles and team 24px, needs list and warning 18px, dossier papers 20px, analysis rows 14px). Everything interactive is a full pill; every icon container is a circle. The About band uses a fluid 28 to 48px radius. Dashed 1px borders mark provisional content (placeholder tag, team slots and avatars, noscript fallback).

### Named Rules
**The Concentric Rule.** Inner radius equals outer radius minus the bezel. Change `--bezel` or `--radius-shell` together and let `--radius-core` follow.

## Components

### Buttons
Pills with a button inside the button.
- **Shape:** full pill, 48px tall (40px small, 56px large).
- **Primary:** accent fill, on-accent 14px Archivo 600 at 106%, accent-ink border at 0.35 alpha, button lift. Hover moves to accent-hover. The trailing icon sits in a 32px orb (white at 0.14) tucked into the right edge.
- **Ghost:** white at 0.04 over a hairline-strong border, title-ink label; hover brightens fill and border.
- **Light:** title-ink fill with navy label and an accent orb; used only on the accent CTA block, hover to pure white.
- **Hover / Press:** the orb escapes diagonally (2px, -1px, scale 1.06); press scales to 0.97. CTAs marked magnetic follow the pointer slightly via the CSS `translate` property. All movement is off under reduced motion.

### Chips
- **Style:** pill, 40px, translucent surface (0.55) over hairline-strong, body-ink 14px 500 label with a 16px accent-ink icon. Hover lights to surface with an accent-ink border. The "more" chip is transparent with accent-ink text.
- **Use:** the hero "O que aconteceu com você?" shortcuts that open service sheets; vestige chips on the navy tile.
- **Status pill:** small accent-tinted pill with a dot, accent-ink text.

### Floating WhatsApp
Graphite glass pill (0.85, 14px blur, float shadow), icon only on mobile and labeled from 1024px; appears after scrolling, hides while a sheet is open.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** build containers as double bezels with concentric radii (28px shell, 6px bezel, 22px core).
- **Do** make every interactive control a pill and every icon container a circle; put icons in navy discs with accent-ink glyphs.
- **Do** spend navy and accent as whole fields, and swap in the navy-scoped ink and redaction bar inside navy.
- **Do** keep atmosphere to the fixed navy and accent light fields behind content.
- **Do** use `--ease-fluid` (cubic-bezier(0.32, 0.72, 0, 1)) for entrances and morphs and `--ease-out` (cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)) for presses; entrances rise 40px out of a 10px blur.
- **Do** gate every moving effect behind the `motion-ok` class and `prefers-reduced-motion`, keeping color and opacity states.
- **Do** mark unconfirmed facts with the dashed placeholder pill rather than inventing values.

### Don't:
- **Don't** add grid, grain, noise or any surface texture.
- **Don't** glow anything except the hero primary CTA.
- **Don't** set headings, body, buttons, section labels or data values in Martian Mono.
- **Don't** introduce new hues; WhatsApp green stays on its icon.
- **Don't** use black or hard-edged shadows; shadows are wide, soft and navy-tinted.
- **Don't** use neon, glitch, green code, skulls, padlocks, hooded figures, law-office serifs or cold corporate-bank styling.
- **Don't** put grey ink directly on navy fields.

## Componentes da versão 3.0

Todos usam só as variáveis de `css/variables.css`, então funcionam nos dois temas. Os
estilos ficam em `css/ferramentas.css` (peças da página inicial e do menu) e em
`css/paginas.css` (páginas geradas). O movimento segue o nível do resto do site: entradas
de 200 a 400ms com `--ease-out`, nada que pisque ou chame atenção sem motivo.

### Painel de serviços ("mega menu")
Vidro igual ao da ilha (`--header-bg-solid` com 18px de desfoque e borda
`--hairline-strong`), 28px de canto e a mesma largura do conteúdo. Ele tem quatro colunas
de categorias, divididas automaticamente, cada uma com rótulo mono, quantidade em
`accent-ink` e itens com disco navy. A coluna de destaque à direita tem o card "Caí em um
golpe agora", com brilho verde-água no canto e botão principal, e o atalho "Ver todos os

- **Movimento:** desce 8px com fade em 200ms; os grupos entram em sequência de 30ms.
- **Onde editar:** colunas em `js/navegacao.js` (`COLUNAS_DO_PAINEL`); serviços em
  `js/services.js`.

### Botão de urgência
