## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript (no framework, no build step), with the Motion library (vanilla JS API) for animation. Must open by double-clicking `index.html` (file://), so no ES modules and no runtime fetches of local data. Pinned by the user's brief.

## Users

Primary: people in Brazil who have just been victims of a digital scam (romance scam, OLX, fake delivery, fake auction, fake boleto, fake Pix) or who need someone located, assets found for a court judgment, or a social-media case investigated. They usually arrive from a phone, often nervous, ashamed, and already distrustful of anything online because they were just deceived. Secondary: lawyers and creditors who need asset searches for sentence execution.

## Product Purpose

Vestígio (descriptor: Investigação Digital) is a new digital-investigation firm run by three partners, based in Recife/PE with nationwide service (to be confirmed). It gathers and organizes evidence, identifies authors and assets, and accompanies the case alongside police and courts. The site's success is a visitor who trusts the firm enough to open a WhatsApp conversation about their specific case.

## Positioning

Complements, never replaces, police and the courts: the firm does the investigative legwork that overloaded public bodies rarely have capacity for, and turns it into organized, lawful technical reports that feed police and judicial steps.

All contact and document exchange happens on WhatsApp (5581992183600, test number). No forms that collect data on the site. Contact email: contato.vestigioo@gmail.com. Legal services are performed by partner lawyer(s) registered with the OAB.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services are grouped into: Estelionato Digital, Investigações em Redes Sociais, Investigações e Localização, Recuperação e Suporte Jurídico, Privacidade.
- Never promise guaranteed results; use "buscamos", "auxiliamos", "possível". Data removal from the internet depends on analysis and is not guaranteed. Money recovery is not guaranteed.
- Never ask for passwords, verification codes, or account access (stated on every service detail).
- Investigations use lawful sources, within LGPD.
- Undecided: team names and bios, CNPJ, office hours, confirmed service area, pricing.

## Brand Commitments

- Name "Vestígio" as main mark, "Investigação Digital" as descriptor. The name comes from the forensic term for the trace left at a crime scene; the identity may explore that subtly.
- Pinned palette (dark theme): bg #141619, surface #2C2E3A, navy #050A44, accent #0A21C0 (used sparingly), muted text #B3B4BD. Lighter tints may be derived; no new hues except WhatsApp green on its icon only.
- Must not look like: hacker cliché (green code, Matrix rain, skulls, glitch, padlocks), neon/glow, flashy gradients, hooded-man stock photos, a traditional law office, a cold corporate bank, or something overly simple and basic.
- Voice: Brazilian Portuguese, professional, human, welcoming to someone nervous or ashamed.

## Product Principles

1. Trust is earned by honesty: say what is possible, what is not guaranteed, and what we never ask for.
2. Useful before commercial: the visitor should leave knowing what to do next even if they never hire us.
3. Specific beats generic: each service names exactly what we need from the client.
4. Complement the public system, never disparage or replace it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Mobile-first; WCAG AA contrast; visible keyboard focus; semantic HTML and aria labels; `prefers-reduced-motion` respected on every animation. Plain language for lay readers under stress.
